How BLAST works

No staking. No claiming. No team wallet. Fees become blasts. Blasts become burns.

1
Fees stack up

Every trade of $BLAST pays fees to the creator. Nobody touches them. They pile up until the next blast.

2
Every 3 min

The clock hits zero. 100% of the fees get claimed. Not most of it. All of it.

3
BLAST

All the ETH goes into one buy of $BLAST. One green candle into the chart, on the clock, every 3 minutes.

4
Burned

The $BLAST the blast just bought is burned right away. Supply drops. Nobody can ever sell it.

The rules of BLAST

Simple. Every number is live from the engine. Every blast leaves three transactions on Blockscout: claim, blast, burn.
